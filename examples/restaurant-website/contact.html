FAQ

Common questions

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes! We keep the bar and chef's counter for walk-ins every night, first come, first served.

Do you cater to dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Our menu marks vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and the kitchen can adapt most plates. Please mention allergies when booking.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. Come as you are, just no beachwear please.

Are children welcome?

Yes, we have high chairs and a small kids' menu. Let us know in your reservation notes.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, in any amount from ฿1,000, available in the restaurant or by email.

Our Story

A small kitchen with a big fire.

Ember & Sage began as a 20-seat supper club run by Chef Mali Srisuk and her brother Arun. A decade later, the hearth is bigger — but the idea is the same: cook honest food, simply, over fire.

We work with farmers we know by name, bake our bread every morning and make every pasta by hand.

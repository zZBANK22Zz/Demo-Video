Our Story

From a 20-seat supper club to the old town's favorite hearth

In 2014, siblings Mali and Arun Srisuk hosted weekend dinners in a converted shophouse with one wood oven and borrowed chairs. Word spread, the waiting list grew, and the supper club became Ember & Sage.

Today we seat 80 guests, but we still cook the same way: seasonal produce, open flame, and nothing on the plate that doesn't need to be there.

What We Believe

Our principles

Seasonal First

Our menu follows the farms, not the other way around. If it's not in season, it's not on the plate.

Zero Waste Kitchen

Trimmings become stocks, ferments and staff meals. We compost the rest with our partner farms.

Hospitality Always

Great food is half the evening. We want every guest to feel like a regular from the first visit.
